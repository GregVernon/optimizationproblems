# Golden section search constant

## Description of constant

[Clear mathematical description of the constant $C_N$ one wishes to bound, where $N$ is the number of the file.]

The bisection method is a simple method for finding the extremum of a unimodal function on a bounded domain.
While simple to implement its convergence is slow.
A modification[1], known as the *golden section search*, is minimax optimal 
"in the sense that no matter where the peak may happen to be, 
the final interval within which the peak will be known with certainty to lie will be as small as possible." [2]
Given two bounds, $x_{\min}$ and $x_{\max}$, a new guess is made according to either 
$x = x_{\min} + C_{11} \left( x_{\max} - x_{\min} \right)$ or $x = x_{\max} - C_{11} \left( x_{\max} - x_{\min} \right)$.
The method proceeds according to the rules of the bisection method (which uses a constant of $\frac{1}{2}$ rather than $C_{11}$).

The method is referred to as the *golden section search* as it is equivalent to the "extreme and mean ratio" defined by Euclid[4], 
with $C_{11}$ being the ratio $AC \ : \ AB$.
The ratio $AC \ : \ CB$ is sometimes referred to as the *golden ratio*, $\phi \approx 1.618\dots$[5].
